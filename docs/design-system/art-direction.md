# Nova Commerce — Art Direction v1.0

## Brand Personality

Nova Commerce is a premium, modern ecommerce brand that blends the minimal elegance of Apple with the friendliness of Shopify and the editorial quality of Aesop.

The website should feel:

- Premium
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Minimal
- Aspirational

Never flashy.

Never cheap.

Every image must feel like it belongs to the same brand.

---

# Photography Style

Commercial product photography.

Apple-level product presentation.

Luxury ecommerce photography.

Studio lighting.

Editorial composition.

Ultra realistic.

No CGI appearance.

No exaggerated reflections.

No oversaturated colors.

Natural premium materials.
